# PS-Agents


PEAS Framework for  Searching Agent


Performance Measure: Minimize fuel consumption, comfort of the passenger by their rating, shortest route taken, mnimize time taken to move form source to destination


Environment: Variety of murram and tarmacked roads, Roads with other vehicles, pedestrians, obstacles such as potholes, slippery roads, animals


Actuators: output to a display screen or voice to passengers, accelerator, steering and braking


Sensors: CCTVs, Gps to capture real time location on the map, Speedometer, Infrared to detect obtsacles and distance between other cars, Keyboard or microphone as input to take the location of the passenger
